Engineering formulas

⚙️

Shaft power

P = T · ω

Torque times angular velocity. ω = 2πn/60 for n in rpm.

🔩

Normal stress

σ = F / A

Axial force over cross-sectional area. 1 MPa = 1 N/mm².

📏

Hooke's law

σ = E · ε

Linear elastic region. Steel: E ≈ 200 GPa.

⚡

Kinetic energy

E = ½ · m · v²

Mass in kg, velocity in m/s gives energy in joules.

🔥

Heat transfer

Q = m · c · ΔT

Water: c ≈ 4.186 kJ/(kg·K).

🌍

Standard gravity

g = 9.80665 m/s²

1 kgf = 9.80665 N. 1 atm = 101 325 Pa.
